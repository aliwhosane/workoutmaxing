Workout Maxing

Privacy & Support

Last updated 1 September 2026

Workout Maxing keeps your training data on your phone. You can use every part of the app without an account. If you choose to sign in, your data is also stored on our server so your other devices can see it.

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and there is no analytics or tracking in the app.

What is stored on your device

Everything, by default: your workouts, the sets you log, the plans you follow, your progression state and your unit preferences. This lives in a database on your phone and never leaves it unless you sign in.

What is stored on our server

Only if you sign in. In that case we hold:

  • Your account identifier from Apple or Google, and your email address if the provider gives us one.
  • A device record — a random identifier, the platform (iOS or Android) and when it was last seen. This exists so you can sign out of a lost phone.
  • Your training data — workouts, sets, plans, enrolments and progression.

We never receive your password. Apple and Google verify who you are and send us a signed token; we check that token and issue our own session.

Health data

If you turn on the Apple Health or Health Connect toggle in Settings, the app:

  • Writes the workouts you finish, so they appear alongside your other activity.
  • Reads your bodyweight, to show strength relative to it.

Health data is used only for those two purposes. It is never sent to our server, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone. You can revoke access at any time in Apple Health or Health Connect, and the app continues to work.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No location collection.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account erases everything we hold on the server — your account, your device records and every row of training data — behind two confirmations, with no need to contact anyone.

Your training history stays on the phone when you do that. Deleting the account is a decision about syncing, not about throwing away a training log. Signing out instead simply stops syncing and removes the session from the device. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
